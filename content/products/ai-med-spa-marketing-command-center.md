## The 90-minute weekly workflow

- **Monday (45 min):** Copy.ai batch — treatment page refresh, one email campaign, Instagram caption set, review responses. Canva — one promo graphic + one carousel from the batch.
- **Wednesday (20 min):** Synthesia — one patient-education or promo video. No camera, no editing.
- **Friday (25 min):** AdCreative.ai — refresh or extend the active seasonal campaign. Speechify — convert one piece of content (guide, FAQ, newsletter) to audio.

## The 10 production checklists

1. **Monday content batch** — inputs, prompts, QA steps, publish targets.
2. **Wednesday video** — script approval, avatar/brand template, caption and thumbnail steps.
3. **Friday ads** — variant review, medical-director sign-off, budget check, launch steps.
4. **Review-response routine** — daily 10-minute loop for new reviews.
5. **Compliance pre-publish** — claims checklist, photo-consent check, platform policy scan.
6. **Email campaign** — list segment, sequence selection, send windows, tracking links.
7. **Seasonal promo launch** — 3-week runway from creative to final-day urgency.
8. **Membership campaign** — pitch page, email, ad, and front-desk script touchpoints.
9. **Quarterly content audit** — what produced bookings, what to retire, what to double.
10. **Monthly analytics review** — consult requests, email revenue, ad cost per lead, video watch-through.
