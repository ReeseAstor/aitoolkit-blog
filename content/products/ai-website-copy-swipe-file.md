## Sample Prompts (Preview)

### Prompt #1: Hero Headline Generator

```
You are a conversion copywriter. Write 5 homepage hero headlines for [BUSINESS_NAME], a [BUSINESS_TYPE] that helps [TARGET_AUDIENCE] achieve [PRIMARY_BENEFIT].

Rules:
- Each headline must be under 10 words
- Lead with the outcome, not the feature
- Use active voice
- Include at least one headline that addresses an objection
- Include at least one headline with a specific number or timeframe

Our biggest differentiator is [UNIQUE_MECHANISM].
Our customers' biggest frustration before finding us is [PAIN_POINT].

Output format: numbered list, with a 1-sentence explanation of the angle for each.
```

### Prompt #13: Lead Magnet Landing Page

```
Write a complete landing page for a free [LEAD_MAGNET_TYPE] titled "[LEAD_MAGNET_TITLE]".

The target audience is [TARGET_AUDIENCE] who struggle with [PAIN_POINT].
The lead magnet solves this by [WHAT_IT_DELIVERS].
After downloading, the next step in our funnel is [NEXT_STEP].

Write:
1. Headline (benefit-driven, under 12 words)
2. Subheadline (expand on the promise, address skepticism)
3. 3 bullet points of what they'll learn/get
4. Form field labels (make them feel low-friction)
5. Button copy (action-oriented, not "Submit")
6. Privacy microcopy (one line below the button)
7. Social proof line (e.g., "Join X others")

Tone: [conversational/professional/bold].
```

### Prompt #25: Founder Origin Story

```
Write a compelling "About" page origin story for [FOUNDER_NAME], founder of [BUSINESS_NAME].

Key facts:
- Previous career/role: [BEFORE]
- Turning point: [WHAT_HAPPENED]
- First version of the business: [EARLY_DAYS]
- Current impact: [RESULTS_NOW]

Structure using the Hero's Journey:
1. The Ordinary World (where they started)
2. The Call to Adventure (what changed)
3. The Ordeal (the hardest moment)
4. The Reward (what they built and who it helps now)

Tone: authentic, not corporate. Should feel like a real person telling their story over coffee, not a LinkedIn "About" section.

Length: 300-400 words. End with a transition to what the company does today.
```

## Bonus: Copy Evaluation Checklist

Use this 10-point checklist to score your AI-generated copy before publishing:

| # | Check | Pass? |
|---|-------|-------|
| 1 | Headline leads with a benefit, not a feature | ☐ |
| 2 | Subheadline addresses the #1 objection | ☐ |
| 3 | Every section answers "So what?" | ☐ |
| 4 | Specific numbers replace vague claims | ☐ |
| 5 | CTA tells them exactly what happens next | ☐ |
| 6 | Social proof is above the first CTA | ☐ |
| 7 | "You" appears more than "We" or "I" | ☐ |
| 8 | No jargon your customer wouldn't use | ☐ |
| 9 | Mobile preview: first screen has a clear CTA | ☐ |
| 10 | Reads aloud naturally (no robot voice) | ☐ |
