Synthesia: AI Avatar Video Production

Synthesia is the anchor of any AI product demo stack. It replaces the most expensive and time-consuming part of traditional video production: the on-camera presenter. Instead of hiring an actor, renting a studio, and scheduling a shoot, you type a script, select an avatar, and generate a professional presenter video in minutes.

For product demos specifically, Synthesia's Starter plan at $19/mo (billed monthly) includes 10 minutes of video per month, 125+ stock AI avatars, custom on-brand avatars, AI dubbing, and the ability to remove the Synthesia watermark. The Creator plan at $89/mo bumps you to 30 minutes per month, adds 5 personal avatars, branded video pages, API access, interactive videos, and multiple avatars per scene. For teams producing demos at scale, Enterprise offers unlimited video minutes, 240+ stock avatars, unlimited personal avatars, SCORM export for LMS integration, and 1-click translation into 80+ languages.

The practical workflow for a product demo: write your script in Copy.ai, paste it into Synthesia scene-by-scene, select an avatar that matches your brand tone, add screen recordings as background elements, and generate. A three-minute demo with four scenes takes roughly 30-45 minutes from script to rendered video. Re-shooting when the UI changes means editing the script text and regenerating, not rebooking a studio.

In our testing, the AI avatars are convincing enough for B2B SaaS demos, onboarding videos, and explainer content. They are not yet at the level where you would mistake them for a human in a close-up cinematic shot, but for the typical product demo format (presenter in a corner or full-screen with slides), the quality is production-ready.

Copy.ai: Demo Scripts and Voiceover Copy

Every good product demo starts with a tight script, and Copy.ai is the fastest way to get from a feature brief to a structured, scene-by-scene demo script. The Chat plan at $24/mo (billed annually at $288/yr) or $29/mo monthly includes unlimited words in chat, 5 seats, unlimited chat projects, and access to OpenAI, Anthropic, and Gemini models.

The workflow: paste your product's feature list, target audience, and key differentiators into Copy.ai. Ask it to write a three-minute product demo script with scene directions, presenter dialogue, and on-screen text cues. Then iterate: tighten the hook, adjust the tone for your audience (technical buyers versus executive sponsors), and generate a separate voiceover narration version for screen-capture demos where no avatar is on screen.

Copy.ai also handles the supporting copy that surrounds the video: the YouTube description, the landing page section where the demo embeds, the email sequence that nurtures leads after they watch, and the social media captions that promote the video across platforms. One tool covers the entire text layer of your video production pipeline.

For teams producing demos at volume, the Growth plan at $1,000/mo adds 75 seats and 20,000 workflow credits per month, enabling automated script generation from product changelogs and feature release notes. Most solo creators and small teams will never need to leave the Chat tier.

Canva: Thumbnails, Graphics, and Social Cutdowns

A product demo video without a strong thumbnail gets buried. Canva Pro at $144/yr handles the entire visual layer around your video: YouTube thumbnails, lower-third name bars, branded intro and outro cards, social media cutdowns (square for Instagram, vertical for TikTok and Reels, landscape for LinkedIn), and the landing page banner where the demo lives.

Canva Pro unlocks 3.6M+ premium templates, 141M+ stock assets, 5 Brand Kits for consistent colors and fonts, Magic Resize for instant cross-platform reformatting, and the social content planner for scheduling promotional posts. The AI tools (Magic Write, Magic Edit, background remover, and the new Canva AI 2.0) let you generate thumbnail concepts from a text prompt and iterate without touching a design tool manually.

For the product demo workflow specifically, Canva's role is post-production and distribution. After Synthesia renders the video, you import key frames into Canva, add branded overlays and text callouts, resize for each platform, and export. The Business plan at $250/yr per person adds 100 Brand Kits, team collaboration, and access to Leonardo.Ai and Flourish for advanced visual work, but Pro covers everything a solo creator or small team needs.

Speechify: Natural AI Voiceover Narration

Not every product demo needs an on-camera avatar. Screen-capture walkthroughs with voiceover narration remain the most common format for software demos, tutorial content, and feature announcements. Speechify Premium at $29/mo provides 1,000+ natural-sounding AI voices across 60+ languages, listening speeds up to 5x, and integrations with Google Drive, Dropbox, and Microsoft OneDrive.

The workflow: take the voiceover script you generated in Copy.ai, paste it into Speechify, select a voice that matches your brand (professional, warm, energetic), and export the audio track. Layer it over your screen recording in any video editor. The result sounds like a professional voice actor at a fraction of the cost and turnaround time. Annual billing saves 60% compared to the monthly rate.

Speechify also handles accessibility: adding narrated audio to your demo video makes it consumable by visually impaired users and by anyone who prefers audio over video. The AI Podcasts feature can even convert your demo transcript into a podcast-style audio clip for distribution on audio platforms.

For teams that need programmatic voiceover generation at scale, Speechify offers a separate Studio product and API with pay-as-you-go pricing, but the Premium plan covers individual creators and small teams producing a few demos per month.

AdCreative.ai: Promo Ads for Video Distribution

A great product demo video that nobody sees is a wasted investment. AdCreative.ai generates the paid advertising creative that drives targeted traffic to your demo video. The Starter plan at $39/mo includes 10 credits per month for generating conversion-optimized ad designs sized for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Google Display, and other platforms.

The practical application: after your demo video is published, feed AdCreative.ai your video thumbnail, key value proposition, and target audience. It generates multiple ad creative variations scored by predicted conversion performance. You pick the top performers, load them into your ad platform, and drive qualified traffic to the video landing page.

One important caveat: the Starter plan at $39/mo is image-only. Video ad creative, storytelling ads, and batch creative generation are locked behind the Professional plan at $249/mo. If your promotion strategy relies on video ads (short clips from the demo itself), budget for Professional. If static image ads driving to the video landing page are sufficient, Starter works well and keeps the total stack cost under $130/month.

The Full Product Demo Workflow

Here is the complete pipeline from feature brief to published, promoted product demo:

  1. Script (Copy.ai, 30 min): Paste your feature list and target audience into Copy.ai. Generate a scene-by-scene demo script with presenter dialogue, on-screen text cues, and a separate voiceover narration version.
  2. Presenter Video (Synthesia, 45 min): Paste the script into Synthesia, select an avatar, add screen recordings as scene backgrounds, and generate the presenter video. Export in 1080p.
  3. Voiceover (Speechify, 15 min): For screen-capture sections without an avatar, generate natural AI narration from the voiceover script. Export the audio track and sync with your screen recording.
  4. Visual Assets (Canva, 30 min): Create the YouTube thumbnail, lower-third name bars, branded intro/outro cards, and social media cutdowns in three aspect ratios (16:9, 1:1, 9:16).
  5. Publish and Promote (AdCreative.ai, 20 min): Generate ad creative variations from your video thumbnail and key messaging. Launch paid campaigns on Facebook, LinkedIn, or Google Display driving to the demo landing page.

Total time: approximately two to three hours. Total monthly cost for the full stack: $155/month if paying monthly ($19 + $29 + $29 + $39 + Canva at $12/mo effective), or roughly $130/month with annual billing on Copy.ai and Canva.
