What Is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

GEO is the practice of getting your brand mentioned and cited inside AI-generated answers — from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and Google's AI Overviews — rather than (or alongside) ranking in traditional search results. Where SEO optimizes for ranking position, GEO optimizes for share of voice: how often your brand appears, in what context, and which sources the AI cites when it recommends you.

The mechanics differ from SEO in three ways that matter for your tool choices:

  • AI engines cite sources, not just rank them. Perplexity and Google AI Overviews pull citations from indexed pages. ChatGPT and Gemini draw on training data plus live search. Getting cited means having structured, authoritative, easily-extractable content that these engines can quote.
  • Measurement is prompt-based, not keyword-based. You track whether your brand appears when the AI answers a set of category prompts ("best CRM for freelancers," "how to automate invoicing"), across multiple AI platforms, refreshed regularly.
  • The feedback loop is slower and fuzzier. You cannot "submit" a page to ChatGPT. You improve your content, earn citations, and measure whether AI answers shift over weeks.

That means your GEO stack needs three capabilities: tracking (are we cited, where, versus whom), optimization (is our content structured to be cited), and production (can we publish enough citable content to move the needle). Here is the tool for each.

The 90-Day GEO Workflow

Here is how the four tools fit together across a typical GEO push:

Days 1-14 (Baseline): Set up Writesonic to track your brand across 20-30 category prompts. Identify the prompts where competitors are cited and you are absent. Pull the same prompts into Surfer to see what the cited pages have that yours lack.

Days 15-45 (Fix & Produce): Rewrite your 5-10 highest-priority pages in Surfer's Content Editor, targeting a Content Score of 80+. Use Copy.ai to draft net-new pages answering the prompts where you have no content at all. Run every draft through Jasper's brand voice if consistency across writers is a concern.

Days 46-90 (Measure & Iterate): Watch Writesonic's daily tracking for movement on your target prompts. Double down on the content types that shift AI answers. Expand production (Copy.ai for volume, Jasper for brand-critical pages) into the next tier of prompts.
