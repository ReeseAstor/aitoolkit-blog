The 4-Step Workflow: Write a Full Website in Under 2 Hours

Here's the exact process we use to write homepage, landing page, about page, and service page copy from scratch using AI. This works whether you're building a new site from zero or rewriting an existing one.

Step 1: Gather Your Inputs (15 minutes)

Before you open your AI tool, write down these 5 pieces of information. Every prompt in our swipe file uses these as fill-in-the-blank variables:

  • Business name: Your company or brand name
  • Target audience: Who you serve (e.g., "B2B SaaS founders with $10K-$50K MRR")
  • Primary benefit: The #1 outcome you deliver (e.g., "cut customer onboarding time from 2 weeks to 2 days")
  • Unique mechanism: What makes your approach different (e.g., "AI-powered automation + human review")
  • Customer pain point: The frustration they feel before finding you (e.g., "I'm drowning in manual data entry and can't scale")

Spend 15 minutes being specific here. Vague inputs = vague output. "We help small businesses grow" is useless. "We help Shopify stores doing $50K-$200K/month reduce cart abandonment by 30% with AI-powered checkout optimization" gives the AI something to work with.

Step 2: Generate Homepage Copy (30 minutes)

Start with your homepage — it's the most important page and sets the tone for everything else. Use these prompts in order:

  1. Hero headline — generate 5 variations, pick the one that leads with your primary benefit
  2. Hero subheadline — expand on the promise, address the #1 objection
  3. Value proposition stack — 3-4 bullet points of what makes you different
  4. Social proof section — testimonials, client logos, or result numbers
  5. FAQ section — top 5 objections answered before they're asked
  6. Final CTA — tell them exactly what happens when they click

Generate 2-3 variations for each section, pick the best structure, and edit for your voice. Don't expect the AI to nail your tone on the first try — it gets you 80% there, you add the final 20%.

Step 3: Write Landing Pages and About Page (45 minutes)

Once your homepage is solid, use the same inputs to generate:

  • Lead magnet landing page — if you're offering a free guide, webinar, or consultation
  • Pricing page — headline, plan descriptions, and comparison table
  • About page — founder origin story using the Hero's Journey framework
  • Service page — pain-point-first structure with deliverables and pricing justification

The prompts in our swipe file are designed to work together — the homepage copy informs the landing pages, which inform the about page. By the time you finish, every page reinforces the same core message.

Step 4: Test and Iterate (30 minutes)

Before you publish, run your copy through this 10-point checklist (we'll cover this in detail below):

  1. Headline leads with a benefit, not a feature
  2. Subheadline addresses the #1 objection
  3. Every section answers "So what?"
  4. Specific numbers replace vague claims
  5. CTA tells them exactly what happens next
  6. Social proof is above the first CTA
  7. "You" appears more than "We" or "I"
  8. No jargon your customer wouldn't use
  9. Mobile preview: first screen has a clear CTA
  10. Reads aloud naturally (no robot voice)

If your copy fails more than 2 of these, regenerate with more specific inputs. If it passes 8+, you're ready to publish.

5 Mistakes That Kill Your AI-Generated Website Copy

Even with great prompts, these mistakes will tank your conversion rate:

Mistake #1: Vague Inputs

If you tell the AI "we help businesses grow," you'll get "Unlock your business potential with our revolutionary solution." If you tell it "we help Shopify stores doing $50K-$200K/month reduce cart abandonment by 30% with AI-powered checkout optimization," you'll get copy that actually converts.

Fix: Spend 15 minutes writing specific inputs before you open your AI tool. The 5 variables we listed in Step 1 (business name, target audience, primary benefit, unique mechanism, pain point) should each be 1-2 sentences, not 1-2 words.

Mistake #2: Publishing First-Draft Output

AI gets you 80% of the way there. The final 20% — your voice, your specific examples, your customer language — is what separates "sounds like AI wrote this" from "this actually converts."

Fix: Generate 2-3 variations, pick the best structure, then edit for your voice. Read it aloud. If it sounds like a robot, rewrite the clunky parts. Add specific customer quotes or result numbers that only you have.

Mistake #3: Feature-Dumping Instead of Benefit-Leading

"Our platform includes AI-powered analytics, real-time dashboards, and automated reporting" is feature-dumping. "See which marketing channels are actually driving revenue — without opening 12 different tools" is benefit-leading.

Fix: For every feature you mention, ask "So what?" until you hit the emotional outcome. "Real-time dashboards" → "So you can see what's working today, not last month" → "So you can double down on winning campaigns before your competitors notice."

Mistake #4: Generic CTAs

"Learn More," "Get Started," and "Contact Us" are the most common — and least effective — CTA buttons on the web. They don't tell the visitor what happens next.

Fix: Your CTA should answer "What do I get when I click this?" Instead of "Get Started," try "Start Your Free 14-Day Trial (No Credit Card)." Instead of "Contact Us," try "Book a 15-Minute Strategy Call."

Mistake #5: Skipping Social Proof

If your homepage doesn't have testimonials, client logos, or result numbers above the first CTA, visitors have no reason to trust your claims. "We're the best" means nothing. "Trusted by 500+ Shopify stores" means something.

Fix: Put social proof in the first 2-3 scrolls of your homepage. Even if you're new, you can use "Founded by [ex-Google engineer]" or "Built on [proven methodology]" as credibility signals.
