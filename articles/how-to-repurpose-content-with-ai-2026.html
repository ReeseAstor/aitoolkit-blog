You spent 6 hours writing that 2,000-word blog post. It gets a few hundred views, then dies in your archive. Sound familiar?

One in-depth article becomes a YouTube video, a podcast episode, an Instagram carousel, a LinkedIn carousel, a Twitter thread, an email sequence, an ad creative, and a lead magnet — all in under two hours of total work.

Step 1: Start With One Hero Piece

Everything starts with a single, in-depth article — your "hero content." This should be:

  • At least 1,500-2,000 words
  • Structured with clear H2/H3 headings
  • Packed with actionable advice, not fluff
  • Written for a specific audience with a specific problem

Think of this article as raw material. Every paragraph is a potential social post. Every section heading is a potential video topic. Every bullet list is a potential infographic. The more structured your hero piece, the easier it is to atomize.

Pro tip: Write your hero content with repurposing in mind. Use short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max), include numbered lists, and add pull-quote-worthy statements throughout. This isn't "writing for SEO" — it's writing for modularity.

Quality Control: Where AI Falls Short

AI repurposing tools are powerful, but they're not set-and-forget. Here's where human review matters most:

  • Factual accuracy: AI can hallucinate statistics or misrepresent your original point. Always verify claims against your source material.
  • Brand voice consistency: AI-generated content can sound generic. Review every piece and add your personal anecdotes, opinions, and humor.
  • Platform-native formatting: What works on LinkedIn doesn't work on Twitter. Adjust line breaks, emoji usage, and hashtag strategies per platform.
  • Video avatar uncanniness: Synthesia's avatars are impressive but not perfect. Watch every video before publishing — minor lip-sync issues can look unprofessional.
  • Audio tone matching: Speechify's AI voices are natural but may not match your brand's personality. Choose carefully from their 1,000+ options.

The 80/20 rule: Let AI do 80% of the work (drafting, formatting, resizing), then spend 20% of your time adding the human touch (personal stories, current events, inside jokes). This is where your content goes from "AI-generated" to "AI-assisted."

Advanced: The Repurposing Flywheel

Once you've mastered the basic workflow, create a flywheel:

  1. Publish hero article on your blog
  2. Repurpose into 10+ formats (as above)
  3. Track which format drives the most engagement per platform
  4. Double down on top performers next week
  5. Use engagement data to inform your next hero article topic

For example, if your Twitter threads consistently outperform your LinkedIn posts, invest more time in thread quality and less in LinkedIn formatting. If your Synthesia videos get more shares than your Canva carousels, shift your Synthesia plan up to Creator tier.

The data tells you where your audience lives. The AI tools let you serve them there without burning out.
