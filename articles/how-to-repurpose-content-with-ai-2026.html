July 10, 2026 · 10 min read

You spent 6 hours writing that in-depth blog post. It's published, it's ranking, and now it's sitting there — collecting dust while you start from scratch on tomorrow's social media content. Sound familiar?

In this playbook, we tested five AI tools specifically for content repurposing workflows: Copy.ai, Synthesia, Speechify, Canva, and Writesonic. We'll show you exactly which tool handles which repurposing job, how much each costs in 2026, and how to chain them together for maximum output from minimum input.

Copy.ai: Text-to-Text Repurposing

Copy.ai has evolved from a simple headline generator into a full GTM (Go-To-Market) AI platform. For content repurposing, its strongest feature is Workflows — you paste your blog post, select a template (Twitter thread, LinkedIn post, email sequence, Instagram caption), and it generates platform-specific variations in seconds.

What it does well for repurposing:

  • Turns a 2,000-word blog post into 10 social media captions optimized for each platform
  • Generates email subject lines + body copy from article key points
  • Creates ad copy variations (Facebook, Google, LinkedIn) from product descriptions
  • Summarizes long-form content into bullet points, key takeaways, or quote cards text

Pricing in 2026:

The Chat plan costs $24/month billed annually (or $29/month billed monthly). It includes 5 seats, unlimited words in chat, and access to OpenAI, Anthropic, and Gemini models. For most solo creators and small teams, this is more than enough for daily repurposing.

Synthesia: Blog-to-Video Repurposing

Video is the most time-consuming format to produce — and the most expensive to outsource. Synthesia eliminates both problems by generating professional videos with AI avatars that read your content. No camera, no studio, no editing skills required.

What it does well for repurposing:

  • Converts blog posts into narrated explainer videos with realistic AI presenters
  • Creates training videos from internal documentation or how-to articles
  • Generates short-form video clips (vertical format) for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts from article sections
  • Supports 140+ languages — repurpose English content into multilingual video with one click

Pricing in 2026:

The Starter plan costs $29/month and includes basic AI avatars and video generation. The Creator plan at $89/month adds custom avatars, brand kits, and more video minutes. For repurposing 2-4 blog posts per month into video, the Starter plan handles the volume.

Realistic expectations:

Synthesia videos look professional but not indistinguishable from human-presented content. Viewers can tell it's AI-generated — but for explainer content, tutorials, and product walkthroughs, audiences don't mind. Our testing showed similar engagement rates to faceless YouTube channels using stock footage.

The Full AI Repurposing Workflow

Here's how to chain these tools together for maximum output from a single 2,000-word blog post:

Step 1: Start with Copy.ai (15 minutes)

Paste your blog post into Copy.ai's Chat interface. Ask it to generate:

  • 5 Twitter/X thread hooks from your main arguments
  • 3 LinkedIn post variations (storytelling, data-driven, contrarian angle)
  • 1 email newsletter summary with 3 key takeaways
  • 10 quote-worthy sentences for social graphics

Step 2: Create visuals with Canva (20 minutes)

Take the quote-worthy sentences from Step 1 and:

  • Generate 5 Instagram quote cards using Canva's templates
  • Build 1 infographic from your article's key statistics
  • Design 1 Pinterest pin with an AI-generated headline
  • Create a carousel post (5-7 slides) from your article's main sections

Step 3: Produce video with Synthesia (30 minutes)

Select your article's strongest section (the one most likely to perform as standalone content) and:

  • Paste it into Synthesia as a video script
  • Choose an AI avatar that matches your brand tone
  • Add your logo and brand colors
  • Generate a 2-3 minute explainer video
  • Export in vertical format for Reels/Shorts and horizontal for YouTube

Step 4: Create audio with Speechify (15 minutes)

Upload your full blog post to Speechify:

  • Select a natural-sounding AI voice
  • Generate a full audio version for podcast distribution
  • Create shorter audio clips (60-90 seconds) for social media audio posts
  • Export MP3 files for upload to your podcast host or website

Step 5: Optimize with Writesonic (10 minutes)

Use Writesonic to create SEO-optimized variations:

  • Rewrite your article for a secondary keyword (targeting a related search intent)
  • Generate meta descriptions for each format
  • Create landing page copy if the content supports a lead magnet

Total output from one blog post:

  • 5 Twitter threads
  • 3 LinkedIn posts
  • 1 email newsletter edition
  • 5 Instagram quote cards
  • 1 infographic
  • 1 Pinterest pin
  • 1 carousel post (5-7 slides)
  • 1 explainer video (horizontal + vertical)
  • 1 full podcast audio
  • 2-3 short audio clips
  • 1 SEO-optimized article variation

Total time: ~90 minutes. That's 20+ pieces of content from a single blog post.

When NOT to Repurpose

Repurposing works best when your source content is strong. Don't repurpose mediocre content — fix it first. Also, some content types don't translate well:

  • Highly technical documentation — often needs human review after AI repurposing
  • Time-sensitive news content — the repurposing overhead may not justify the short shelf life
  • Personal narrative / opinion pieces — AI-generated variations can feel inauthentic

The sweet spot is evergreen, educational content with clear structure: how-to guides, listicles, comparison articles, and tutorials. These translate naturally across formats and maintain value over months or years.
