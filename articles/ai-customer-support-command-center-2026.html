How the 90-Minute Workflow Works

The core of the product is the 90-minute help center content workflow. Here is what it looks like in practice:

  1. Identify the deflection opportunity (5 min). When the same question hits the inbox 3+ times in a week, copy the three ticket threads. This is your source material.
  2. Draft the article with Copy.ai (15 min). Paste the ticket threads into Copy.ai using Prompt 1 from the product. Get a structured help article with H1 question, one-paragraph answer, numbered steps, troubleshooting, and related articles. Edit for product accuracy.
  3. Capture and annotate screenshots with Canva Pro (20 min). Take 3-5 screenshots of the product flow, drop them into Canva's annotated screenshot template (Prompt 16), add numbered circles and arrow callouts in your brand colors, export as PNG.
  4. Optimize for Google with Surfer SEO (15 min). Run the target query through Surfer (Prompt 32), follow the content brief to hit the target word count and keyword density, confirm the Content Score is 75+ before publishing.
  5. Produce the video version with Synthesia (15 min). Paste the article's step-by-step walkthrough into Synthesia as a script (Prompt 21), pick an avatar, generate a 60-90 second AI-narrated walkthrough. Embed it above the written steps.
  6. Generate the audio version with Speechify (5 min). Paste the published article into Speechify (Prompt 26), pick a natural voice, generate the audio, embed the "Listen to this article" player below the video.
  7. Publish and track deflection (10 min). Publish the article to the help center, add the URL to your support macro for that question, and tag the article with the original ticket IDs so you can measure deflection over the next 30 days.

Run that workflow once a week, and in six months you have a 25-article help center that ranks in Google, serves audio-first customers, includes video walkthroughs, and deflects 100-300 tickets per month automatically — produced by the support team you already have.
