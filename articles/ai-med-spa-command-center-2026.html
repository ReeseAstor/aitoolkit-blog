The problem: marketing eats your treatment hours

Most med spa owners we talk to spend 10-15 hours per week on marketing — treatment pages, email campaigns, Instagram content, seasonal ads, review responses — and still don't publish consistently. All-in-one platforms like Zenoti, Pabau, and PatientNow automate booking and rebooking reminders, but the actual marketing production still falls on you or a front desk that already has a full-time job. The result: the consult calendar has gaps, seasonal promos slip, and competitors own the "[your city] med spa" search results.

The fix isn't hiring an agency ($2,000-5,000/mo) or learning video editing (20+ hours). It's using AI tools with the right prompts — prompts built for med spa workflows, with compliance guardrails baked in, not generic "write a social post" templates.

The 90-minute weekly workflow

The Command Center is built around a repeatable weekly cycle:

  • Monday (45 min): Copy.ai generates the week's treatment page refresh, one email campaign, and a 12-caption Instagram batch. Canva produces one promo graphic and one carousel.
  • Wednesday (20 min): Synthesia generates an AI avatar patient-education video. No camera, no editing.
  • Friday (25 min): AdCreative.ai refreshes the active seasonal campaign. Speechify converts one piece of content — a treatment guide, FAQ, or newsletter — to audio.

That's it. That's the whole marketing department. 90 minutes per week, every week, even during a full consult week.

Compliance built in, not bolted on

Med spa marketing follows stricter rules than most local businesses: platform health-ad policies, FTC claims rules, FDA rules for drug-adjacent claims, and state medical board guidance. The Command Center bakes the guardrails into the prompts themselves — no guarantees, no outcome promises, no before/after imagery without signed consent, medical-director review before any ad launches. The pack is a marketing production system, not medical or regulatory advice; it tells you where the guardrails sit, your medical director tells you what passes through them.
