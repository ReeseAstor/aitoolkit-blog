The Command Center is built around a repeatable weekly cycle:

  • Monday (45 min): Copy.ai generates 5 LinkedIn posts from one tax theme. Canva produces a tax checklist or deadline calendar. One client email drafted and queued.

  • Friday (25 min): AdCreative.ai refreshes underperforming ad creatives. Speechify converts a tax guide into audio for clients.

That's it. That's the whole marketing department. 90 minutes per week, every week, even during busy season.
