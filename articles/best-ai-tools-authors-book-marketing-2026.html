Copy.ai: Launch Emails, Blurbs, Social Captions, and Ad Copy

Book marketing is a writing problem before it is anything else. Back-cover blurbs, Amazon A+ content, launch email sequences, ARC reader outreach, BookTok captions, BookBub ad copy, newsletter swaps, press releases — it is all text, and it all has to sound like you and sell the book. Copy.ai handles the full text production pipeline for an author launch.

The Chat plan gives you unlimited words across the latest LLMs (GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini) for up to 5 seats. The practical author workflow looks like this: paste your book brief (genre, comp titles, logline, protagonist, three-sentence hook, tone notes) into a project, then generate a 7-email launch sequence, 30 social caption variations across BookTok/Instagram/LinkedIn/Facebook, 10 Amazon A+ content blocks, and a one-page press kit in under two hours. The output needs editing — no AI copy is publish-ready raw, and readers can smell generic AI enthusiasm from a mile away — but the first-draft time savings is 8-10x compared to staring at a blank page. For a fiction author, that means prompts like "write a BookTok hook for a slow-burn enemies-to-lovers romance set in a Manhattan penthouse" produce usable raw material you then sharpen with your actual voice.

2026 Pricing: Chat plan is $29/month ($24/month billed annually, $288/year) for 5 seats with unlimited chat words. The workflow-automation tiers (Growth, Expansion, Scale) start at $1,000/month and are built for enterprise sales teams, not authors. The Chat plan is the right tier for book marketing.

Best for: Launch email sequences, back-cover and Amazon blurbs, ARC outreach emails, social captions in your genre's voice, BookBub ad copy variations, press releases, newsletter swap content, reader-magnet landing page copy.

Canva: Social Graphics, Quote Cards, Cover Reveals, and Promo Banners

Book marketing is relentlessly visual. Cover reveals, quote cards pulled from your strongest lines, countdown graphics, "out now" banners, signed-copy giveaway graphics, bookstagram flat-lays, newsletter headers, BookBub ad images, convention table banners — the list does not end. Canva is the fastest way to produce all of it without hiring a designer at $50-150/hour.

Canva Pro unlocks 3.6 million+ templates (including a deep bench of book-promo templates for every genre), 141 million+ premium stock assets, 5 Brand Kits (lock in your author brand colors, fonts, and logo so every graphic matches your cover), background remover (drop your cover onto lifestyle mockups), Magic Resize (turn one cover-reveal design into BookTok 9:16, Instagram 1:1, Facebook 16:9, and X 2:1 versions instantly), and the Social Content Planner for scheduling a launch-week countdown. The Magic Write AI feature also drafts captions inside the design editor — useful when you are batching 20 quote cards and do not want to switch tools.

2026 Pricing: Canva Pro is $144/year for one person (about $12/month on annual billing), or $18/month billed monthly. Canva Business is $250/year per person and adds team admin controls and 100 Brand Kits — overkill for a solo author, but the right call for a small press or an author-assistant team. The free tier exists but is too limited for a real launch (no background remover, no Brand Kits, no Magic Resize).

Best for: Cover reveal graphics, quote cards from your strongest lines, BookTok/Instagram carousels, countdown graphics, "out now" banners, giveaway graphics, newsletter headers, BookBub ad images, signed-bookplate mockups, convention and bookfair table banners.

Synthesia: Book Trailers, Author Intro Videos, and Talking-Head Promos

Video sells books. A 60-second book trailer on your Amazon author page, your BookBub profile, and pinned to the top of your BookTok can lift conversion 20-40%. An author intro video — "hi, I'm the author, here is why I wrote this book" — builds the parasocial connection that turns one-time buyers into backlist readers. Traditionally this meant a videographer, lighting, a decent microphone, and a weekend of filming. Synthesia replaces all of that with AI avatars.

Write your trailer script (or paste one from Copy.ai), pick an avatar from 125+ options on the Starter plan, choose a background that matches your book's mood, and generate a professional video in under ten minutes. The Creator plan adds 5 Personal Avatars — record yourself once with a phone camera, and Synthesia generates unlimited videos in your own likeness. That means every future book trailer, cover-reveal announcement, and "thank you for 1,000 reviews" video can be produced without setting up a camera again. AI Dubbing translates videos into 80+ languages, which matters if you sell foreign rights or have an international reader base.

2026 Pricing: Synthesia restructured pricing in 2026 around a credits system. Starter is $19/month billed monthly (lower on annual billing) for 1,200 credits per month — enough for roughly 10 minutes of finished video, which covers 6-10 short book trailers or author updates. Creator is $89/month billed monthly (lower on annual) for 3,600 credits (roughly 30 minutes of video), 180+ avatars, 5 Personal Avatars, branded video pages, and API access. Enterprise is custom. For an author producing 2-4 short videos per book launch, Starter is the right tier. If you publish 3+ books per year and want your own avatar on every trailer, Creator pays for itself.

Best for: Book trailers for Amazon/BookBub/BookTok, author intro videos for your website and newsletter, cover-reveal announcements, "behind the book" short videos, multilingual book promos for foreign editions, virtual event and book-club intro videos.

AdCreative.ai: Amazon, BookBub, and Meta Ads for Book Sales

Organic reach on BookTok and Instagram is real but unreliable — one viral video can sell 5,000 copies, and the next ten posts can sell 12. Paid ads on Amazon (sponsored products), BookBub, Meta, and Facebook reader groups are how you turn a launch spike into sustained weekly sales. The bottleneck is creative production: you need 10-20 ad variations per platform, tested against each other, refreshed weekly. AdCreative.ai generates conversion-optimized ad creatives scored by predicted performance, so you can launch a full ad test in an afternoon instead of briefing a designer and waiting a week.

The Starter plan ($39/month) gives you 10 credits and 1 brand — enough for a single book's launch campaign if you are disciplined about testing 3-4 concepts per platform. The Professional plan ($249/month) unlocks video ads, 50 credits, and 3 brands — the right tier for authors managing a backlist of 3+ books or running always-on Amazon ads. One caveat: video ad creatives are locked behind Professional. If you only need static image ads for Amazon sponsored products and BookBub, Starter covers it. If you want video ads for Meta and BookTok, budget for Professional.

2026 Pricing: Starter $39/month (10 credits, 1 brand, image ads only). Professional $249/month (50 credits, 3 brands, video ads unlocked). Ultimate $599/month (100 credits, 10 brands). Annual billing saves roughly 25%.

Best for: Amazon sponsored product ad creatives, BookBub featured deal ads, Meta/Facebook reader-group ads, BookTok spark ad creatives, retargeting ads for newsletter signups, audiobook launch ads on Audible-focused placements.

Speechify: Audiobook Samples, Chapter Previews, and Accessibility Audio

The most underused asset in book marketing is audio. A 3-minute audiobook sample — the first chapter read aloud — converts browsers into buyers at 2-3x the rate of a text excerpt. A podcast-style "author reads the prologue" episode extends your reach to listeners who will never visit your website. And for accessibility, providing audio versions of your newsletter, your launch announcement, and your book description means visually impaired readers get the same experience as everyone else. Speechify converts any text into natural-sounding audio with 1,000+ voices in 60+ languages.

The workflow is simple: pull your strongest opening chapter (or your book description, or your launch announcement), paste it into Speechify, pick a voice that matches your genre — a warm, measured voice for literary fiction, a faster pace for thriller, a brighter tone for rom-com — and export an MP3. Embed it on your Amazon author page, your BookBub profile, your website, and your launch email. For authors without an audiobook deal, Speechify Studio (the creator-focused tier with voice cloning and commercial-use rights) can produce a full self-published audiobook, though that is a bigger project than a marketing sample.

2026 Pricing: Speechify Premium (the consumer tier) is $29/month billed monthly, or $159/year on annual billing — the annual plan is the clear choice for authors, unlocking all voices, unlimited listening, and the Chrome extension for turning research articles into audio while you write. Speechify Studio (voice generation for creators, with commercial rights) starts at $49/month and is the tier to use if you are producing distributable audiobook content rather than marketing samples.

Best for: Audiobook samples for Amazon/BookBub/website, podcast-style chapter previews, author newsletter audio versions, accessibility audio for launch announcements and book descriptions, research reading while drafting your next book.

The 8-Week Book Launch Workflow

Here is how the five tools fit together across a typical launch timeline:

Weeks 1-2 (Pre-Launch Setup): Write your Amazon book description, A+ content blocks, and author bio in Copy.ai. Design your cover-reveal graphic, 10 quote cards, and a countdown template set in Canva. Set up your Brand Kit so every asset matches your cover colors.

Weeks 3-4 (ARC + Early Buzz): Generate ARC reader outreach emails and a 5-email launch sequence in Copy.ai. Produce a 60-second book trailer in Synthesia and embed it on your Amazon author page. Create "cover reveal" and "3 weeks to go" graphics in Canva. Record an audio teaser of the prologue in Speechify and email it to your ARC list.

Weeks 5-6 (Launch Week): Send the launch email sequence (Copy.ai). Run Amazon sponsored product ads and BookBub featured deal ads with 10-15 AdCreative.ai variations. Post daily BookTok content (Canva graphics + Synthesia short videos). Publish a podcast-style "author reads chapter one" episode (Speechify).

Weeks 7-8 (Post-Launch Sustain): Write the "thank you" email and a launch-recap newsletter (Copy.ai). Refresh ad creatives with new quote cards (Canva + AdCreative.ai). Produce a "what readers are saying" video pulling early reviews (Synthesia). Release the audiobook sample wide on your website and BookBub (Speechify). Start drafting the next book — with Speechify reading your research aloud while you outline.
