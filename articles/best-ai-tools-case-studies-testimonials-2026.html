Copy.ai: Interview-to-Narrative Writing

The hardest part of a case study is not the interview — it's turning a 45-minute transcript into a tight 800-word narrative with a clear challenge-solution-result arc. Copy.ai handles this stage better than any tool we tested in 2026.

Copy.ai's Chat plan ($24/mo billed annually, or $29/mo monthly) gives you access to OpenAI, Anthropic, and Gemini models inside a workflow builder. You paste your interview transcript, give it a case study framework (challenge → solution → results → quote), and it generates a structured first draft in under a minute. The output is not just a wall of text — it produces a narrative with headers, pull quotes, and a summary you can drop straight into a template.

Where Copy.ai shines for case studies specifically:

  • Transcript-to-story: Paste a raw Zoom transcript and prompt it with "Write a case study using the challenge-solution-results framework. Include two pull quotes from the customer. Keep it under 800 words." The output is a solid first draft that captures the customer's actual words.
  • Testimonial shortening: Customers give rambling 5-minute answers. Copy.ai condenses them into 2-sentence testimonials that keep the original voice and the key metric.
  • Multi-format output: One transcript can become a full case study, a 3-sentence social proof blurb, a LinkedIn post, and an email teaser — all from the same prompt chain.
  • Interview question generation: Before the call, ask Copy.ai to generate 15 case study interview questions tailored to the customer's industry and the problem your product solved.

The Free tier (2,000 words, 1 seat) is enough to test the workflow on one case study. The Chat plan is where production work happens — unlimited words, 5 seats, and access to the best underlying models.

Canva Pro: Case Study Design and One-Pagers

A well-designed one-pager PDF with the customer's logo, a headline result, a pull quote, and a clean challenge-solution-results layout gets forwarded to decision-makers and attached to sales emails. Canva Pro ($144/yr, or about $12/mo billed annually) is the fastest path from draft to designed case study.

Canva's case study templates are production-ready. Search "case study" in the template library and you get hundreds of one-pager, multi-page, and presentation formats. The Pro plan unlocks the Brand Kit (your fonts, colors, logos saved as defaults), background remover (cut out customer logos from screenshots), and one-click resize (turn a horizontal one-pager into a vertical LinkedIn carousel in two clicks).

The 2026 Canva Pro features that matter for case studies:

  • 3.6M+ templates including hundreds of case study and testimonial layouts
  • 5 Brand Kits on Pro — save your brand colors, fonts, and logos so every case study looks consistent
  • Background Remover — cut customer logos from their website and drop them cleanly into your case study design
  • One-click Resize — turn a case study one-pager into an Instagram post, a LinkedIn carousel, and a sales deck slide in seconds

Canva Business ($250/yr per person) adds team admin, 100 Brand Kits, and 500GB storage if you have a team of case study producers. For a solo marketer or small team, Pro at $144/yr is more than enough.

Speechify: Case Study Audio and Podcast Snippets

Audio is the most overlooked case study format. A potential customer might not read your 2-page PDF, but they'll listen to a 3-minute case study narration on their commute. Speechify ($139/yr for Premium, or Studio plans from ~$49/mo for voice generation) turns your written case studies into natural-sounding audio that you can embed on your case study page, drop into a podcast feed, or attach to a sales email.

Speechify Premium is the consumer plan — it reads PDFs, articles, and documents aloud with high-quality AI voices. For case study production, this means you can take your finished Canva PDF, run it through Speechify, and get an audio version that sounds professional. The cross-device sync means you can listen to your own case studies on your phone to review them before sending to the customer for approval.

For teams that want to generate custom voice content — branded case study narration, podcast intro snippets, or voiceovers in specific tones — Speechify Studio offers 200+ voices and voice cloning capabilities. The Studio plans are credit-based, starting around $49/mo, and include commercial usage rights for the generated audio.

The practical case study audio workflow: write the case study in Copy.ai, design it in Canva, then run the text through Speechify to produce a 2-3 minute audio narration. Embed the audio player on the case study landing page alongside the PDF and video. Some prospects prefer audio — giving them the option increases engagement.

The Full Case Study Workflow: From Interview to Multi-Format Asset

Here is the end-to-end workflow that chains all five tools together. Total time: approximately 2.5 to 3 hours per case study, compared to 15-20 hours without AI.

  1. Pre-interview (15 min): Use Copy.ai to generate 15 case study interview questions tailored to the customer's industry and the specific problem your product solved. Send these to the customer before the call so they can prepare.
  2. The interview (45 min): Record the Zoom call. You need the transcript — Otter.ai, Zoom's built-in transcription, or any transcription tool works. The raw transcript is your raw material.
  3. Narrative draft (20 min): Paste the transcript into Copy.ai. Prompt: "Write an 800-word case study using the challenge-solution-results framework. Include two pull quotes. Emphasize the quantified result." Review and edit the output — it will be 80% there. Add the customer's actual numbers where the AI approximated them.
  4. Testimonial extraction (10 min): Ask Copy.ai to extract three 2-sentence testimonials from the same transcript. These become your social proof blurbs for the website, sales decks, and ad copy.
  5. Design the one-pager (30 min): Drop the edited narrative into a Canva Pro case study template. Add the customer logo (use Background Remover to clean it up). Add a headline result as the visual anchor. Export as PDF.
  6. Produce the video (20 min): Take the key quote and results section, write a 60-second script, paste into Synthesia, select your avatar, and generate. Download the MP4.
  7. Generate audio (10 min): Run the case study text through Speechify to produce a 2-3 minute audio narration. Download the MP3.
  8. Create social proof ads (15 min): Input the headline result and testimonial into AdCreative.ai. Generate 10 ad creative variations in multiple sizes. Pick the top 3 and deploy to your ad channels.
  9. Publish (15 min): Build the case study landing page with the PDF, embedded video, audio player, and testimonial blurbs. Route to the customer for final approval.

The output from one customer interview: one full case study PDF, one 60-second video testimonial, one 3-minute audio narration, three social proof testimonial blurbs, and ten social proof ad creatives. That is six assets from one interview — a 5x to 10x multiplier on what most teams produce.
