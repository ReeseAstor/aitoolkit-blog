Why we built this (and why it is different)

Last week we published our comparison of the five best AI tools for fitness coaches. That article answers "which tools?" This product answers "what do I actually type into them?"

Most AI prompt packs are generic. They give you "write an Instagram caption about fitness" and call it a day. The Command Center is built differently:

  • Every prompt is tool-specific. The Copy.ai prompts are structured for Copy.ai's chat interface. The Canva prompts are design briefs for Magic Studio. The Synthesia prompts include avatar direction, timing marks, and on-screen text cues.
  • Every prompt has bracketed fields. You fill in [YOUR NICHE], [PROGRAM NAME], [CLIENT TYPE] — the AI does the rest. No prompt engineering knowledge required.
  • The checklists create a repeatable weekly rhythm. Monday is content batch day (45 min). Wednesday is video day (20 min). Friday is ads + audio day (25 min). Total: 90 minutes. Every week. Forever.
