The 90-Minute Weekly Sprint in Practice

Here is how the sprint looks for a Shopify store with 20 products and a small Meta ad budget:

  1. 0-15 min: Pick the week's promotion and top 5 SKUs. Open Copy.ai.
  2. 15-45 min: Generate product titles, bullets, one email, and one SMS for each SKU using the prompt templates.
  3. 45-65 min: Drop the copy into Canva. Create one hero image per SKU and Magic Resize it for Instagram, Pinterest, and email.
  4. 65-80 min: In AdCreative.ai, generate 10-20 ad variants for the promoted product and keep only the 70+ conversion-score creatives.
  5. 80-90 min: Queue everything in your email platform, ad manager, and social scheduler.

If you have a hero product that needs video, swap 15 minutes of social graphics for a 60-second Synthesia explainer script and render. You can batch the actual video rendering while you queue the rest.
