Our playbook includes two video prompts: a 60-90 second chef intro / restaurant story video (hook, origin, signature dish, community, CTA) and a 30-45 second seasonal promo video (text cards, dish close-up shots, voiceover, closing CTA). The 90-Minute Weekly Workflow

Here is the workflow in a nutshell. Every Monday, spend 90 minutes following the checklist sequence:

  1. Planning (10 min): Pull this week's specials, events, and promotions. Identify the "angle" of the week.
  2. Batch Writing in Copy.ai (30 min): Run the menu description prompt, the daily specials batch (5 days), the weekly email, and the GBP post.
  3. Visual Production in Canva (25 min): Create 5 daily-special graphics, 2 Story templates, and 1 flyer if needed.
  4. Ad Review (10 min): Check Meta Ads Manager, pause underperformers, generate new creatives if needed.
  5. Video (15 min, bi-weekly): If seasonal menu or event, run the Synthesia prompt and post to Reels + TikTok.

That is one full week of restaurant marketing content — daily social posts, a weekly email, ad creatives, and a video — produced in less time than a single staff meeting.
