Copy.ai: Event Copy, Invitations, and Email Sequences

Every event lives or dies on its copy. Registration pages, invitation emails, speaker bios, session descriptions, sponsor outreach, press releases — it is all text, and it all needs to be persuasive, on-brand, and done yesterday. Copy.ai handles the full text production pipeline for events.

The Chat plan gives you unlimited words across the latest LLMs (GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini) for up to 5 seats. For event teams, the practical workflow looks like this: paste your event brief (dates, venue, audience, ticket tiers, speaker list) into a project, then generate a 5-email registration sequence, 10 social caption variations, 20 session descriptions, and a sponsor prospectus in under an hour. The output needs editing — no AI copy is publish-ready raw — but the first-draft time savings is 6-8x compared to writing from scratch.

2026 Pricing: Chat plan is $29/month ($24/month billed annually, $288/year) for 5 seats with unlimited chat words. Workflow automation tiers (Growth, Expansion, Scale) start at $1,000/month and are built for enterprise GTM teams, not event planners. The Chat plan is the right tier for event marketing.

Best for: Registration page copy, invitation email sequences, session descriptions, speaker bios, sponsor outreach, press releases, post-event thank-you sequences.

Canva: Signage, Social Graphics, Programs, and Booth Design

Events are visual. Wayfinding signs, stage backdrops, social media countdown graphics, printed programs, booth banners, name badges, sponsor logos walls — the list is long and the deadline is immovable. Canva is the fastest way to produce all of it without a designer.

Canva Pro unlocks 3.6 million+ templates, 141 million+ premium stock assets, 5 Brand Kits (lock in your event colors, fonts, and logos so every graphic is consistent), background remover, Magic Resize (turn one design into 15 format variations instantly), and the Social Content Planner for scheduling countdown posts. For a conference, the Brand Kit feature alone saves hours: set it up once, and every team member produces on-brand assets without sending files back and forth.

2026 Pricing: Canva Pro is $180/year (about $15/month) for one person, or $18/month billed monthly. Canva Business (the plan that replaced Teams) is $250/year per person ($25/user/month) and adds 100 Brand Kits, team admin controls, and Leonardo.Ai access. For a solo event planner, Pro is sufficient. For an agency managing multiple client events, Business is the right call.

Best for: Wayfinding signage, stage backdrops, social countdown graphics, printed programs, booth banners, name badges, sponsor walls, email headers, registration page visuals.

Synthesia: Promo Videos, Speaker Intros, and Welcome Messages

Video is the highest-converting asset in event marketing. A 60-second promo video on the registration page can lift conversions 20-35%. Speaker intro videos build anticipation. A personalized welcome message from the event host sets the tone before attendees walk in. Traditionally, this meant a videographer, lighting, editing, and two weeks of turnaround. Synthesia replaces all of that with AI avatars.

Type a script, pick an avatar (125+ on Starter, 180+ on Creator), and generate a professional video in minutes. The Creator plan adds 5 Personal Avatars — record yourself once and generate unlimited videos in your own likeness, which is ideal for event hosts who want a personal touch without re-filming every time. AI Dubbing translates videos into 80+ languages, useful for international conferences.

2026 Pricing: Synthesia recently cut prices. Starter is $19/month billed monthly ($14/month billed annually) for 10 video minutes/month, 125+ avatars, and logo removal. Creator is $89/month billed monthly ($59/month billed annually) for 30 video minutes/month, 180+ avatars, 5 Personal Avatars, API access, and interactive videos. Enterprise is custom. For most event teams producing 3-5 short videos per event, Starter covers it. If you are running monthly events or need your own avatar, Creator is the move.

Best for: Registration page promo videos, speaker introduction clips, attendee welcome messages, session previews, post-event highlight recaps, multilingual promotion for international events.

AdCreative.ai: Paid Social Ads for Ticket Sales

Organic reach is not enough to fill a conference. You need paid ads on Meta, LinkedIn, and Instagram — and you need them fast, in multiple sizes, tested against each other. AdCreative.ai generates conversion-optimized ad creatives scored by predicted performance, so you can launch 10-20 variations in an afternoon instead of waiting a week for a designer.

The Starter plan ($39/month) gives you 10 credits and 1 brand — enough for a single event's ad campaign if you are disciplined. The Professional plan ($249/month) unlocks video ads, 50 credits, and 3 brands, which is the right tier for agencies managing multiple client events. One important caveat: video ad features are locked behind the Professional tier. If you only need static image ads for Meta and LinkedIn, Starter works. If you want video ad creatives, budget for Professional.

2026 Pricing: Starter $39/month (10 credits, 1 brand, image ads only). Professional $249/month (50 credits, 3 brands, video ads unlocked). Ultimate $599/month (100 credits, 10 brands). Annual billing saves roughly 25%.

Best for: Meta/LinkedIn/Instagram ad creatives for ticket sales, retargeting campaigns for past attendees, sponsor promotion ads, early-bird deadline urgency campaigns.

Speechify: Audio Guides, Podcast Recaps, and Accessibility

The most overlooked asset in event marketing is audio. An audio guide attendees can listen to on their commute — "Here is what to expect at Day 1, here are the three sessions you should not miss, here is how to get to the venue" — builds excitement and reduces no-shows. Post-event, a podcast-style recap extends the content's life for months. Speechify converts any text into natural-sounding audio with 200+ voices in 60+ languages.

The workflow is simple: generate your event guide copy in Copy.ai, paste it into Speechify, pick a voice, and export an MP3. Embed it on the registration confirmation page, email it to registered attendees three days before the event, and publish it as a private podcast feed. For accessibility, provide audio versions of all printed materials — programs, speaker bios, session abstracts — so visually impaired attendees get the same experience.

2026 Pricing: Speechify Premium is $11.58/month billed annually ($139/year) or $29/month billed monthly. The annual plan is the clear choice for event teams — it unlocks all voices, unlimited listening, and the Chrome extension.

Best for: Pre-event audio guides, session audio previews, post-event podcast recaps, accessibility audio for printed materials, multilingual audio for international attendees.

The 6-Week Event Marketing Workflow

Here is how the five tools fit together across a typical conference timeline:

Week 1-2 (Announcement): Write the registration page copy, 5-email announcement sequence, and press release in Copy.ai. Design the announcement graphic, social banners, and email header in Canva. Generate a 60-second promo video in Synthesia for the registration page.

Week 3-4 (Promotion): Launch paid ads with 10-15 AdCreative.ai variations across Meta and LinkedIn. Create countdown graphics in Canva. Generate speaker intro videos in Synthesia (one per keynote). Record an audio teaser in Speechify and embed it in email #3.

Week 5 (Final Push): Send the "last chance" email sequence (Copy.ai). Run retargeting ads to past attendees (AdCreative.ai). Publish the full audio guide — venue directions, day-by-day schedule, must-see sessions (Speechify). Print wayfinding signage and programs (Canva).

Week 6+ (Post-Event): Write thank-you emails and a recap blog post (Copy.ai). Create a highlight reel video (Synthesia). Publish a podcast-style recap (Speechify). Design a "save the date" graphic for next year (Canva).
