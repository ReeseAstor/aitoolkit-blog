How to use it

  1. Open the Snapshot section and fill in your current medication, providers, pharmacy, and insurance. Update any time something changes.
  2. For each refill, fill a fresh Refill Cycle Tracker page top to bottom.
  3. Log every pharmacy call and provider message as it happens.
  4. Before every appointment, fill the Prep Sheet. After every appointment, fill the Notes Page before you leave.
  5. If something goes wrong (shortage, missed appointment, lost script), open the matching Reset page.
  6. Once a month, do the Monthly Reset Ritual (10 minutes).
