P03

Quick-Start Guide

Pro Tip

Do this once a month, ideally on the last Sunday of the previous month. Block 90 minutes. Do not skip the brain dump.

Your first 10 minutes:

Open the Brain Dump (next tab). Set a 10-minute timer. Write every content idea you have — titles, topics, half-formed thoughts — one per row. Do not filter. Fill as many rows as you can.
When the timer ends, go to the Batch Day Planner. Group similar ideas onto the same batch day — all talking-head videos on Day 1, all voiceover on Day 2, etc.
Pick your 4–8 strongest pieces. Open a Content Piece Tracker for each one. Fill in the title, platform, hook, and CTA fields. Come back to the rest when you're ready to create.
Put your batch dates in your calendar. You are done planning.
Note

You do not need to fill every page. Use what helps. Leave the rest blank.

Prompt Block Library

30 AI prompt starters across ideas, scripting, and captions. Copy any prompt, fill in the brackets, and paste into your AI tool of choice.

Ideas & Hooks

01 / Ideas
> Generate 10 video/post titles for [topic] that answer a common beginner question.
> Audience: [describe]. Tone: direct, no clickbait.
02 / Hook lines
> Write 5 hook lines for a video about [topic]. Each hook should start with a
> specific situation, not a question. Tone: calm, direct.
03 / Content gaps
> List 8 content ideas for [niche] that I have not covered yet, based on these
> titles I have already made: [paste list].
04 / Objections as angles
> What are the top 3 objections someone has before buying [product or service]?
> Turn each objection into a content angle.
05 / Story hook
> Write a "story hook" opening for a video about [topic]. The viewer should
> recognize themselves in the first sentence.

Scripts & Outlines

06 / Outline
> Write a 3-part outline for a [format: tutorial/story/list] video about [topic].
> Each part should have a clear purpose and transition.
07 / Opening 60s
> Write the opening 60 seconds of a video about [topic]. Include a hook, a brief
> credibility line, and a preview of what they will learn.
08 / Mid-video CTA
> Write a mid-video CTA for [desired action] that does not feel salesy.
> Transition smoothly from the content.
09 / Tighten transcript
> Take this transcript excerpt and tighten it — remove filler words, make sentences
> shorter, keep the voice: [paste excerpt].
10 / Pattern interrupt
> Write a "pattern interrupt" moment for a video that is 3 minutes in and risks
> losing the viewer. Topic: [topic].

Captions & Repurposing

11 / Instagram caption
> Write a 3-sentence Instagram caption for a post about [topic].
> First sentence: bold statement. Second: expand. Third: CTA.
12 / Twitter/X thread
> Turn this video script section into a Twitter/X thread. Each tweet should
> stand alone. 8–12 tweets. Tone: direct, useful. [paste section]
13 / Pinterest descriptions
> Write 5 Pinterest pin descriptions for [topic]. Each under 150 chars.
> Include one keyword and one benefit.
14 / Email newsletter
> Turn this blog outline into an email newsletter. 250 words max.
> Subject line options: 3 variations. [paste outline]
15 / Shorten & direct
> Rewrite this caption to be shorter and more direct.
> Remove: filler phrases, weak adjectives, any hype language. [paste]
